# Product Requirements Document (PRD): RegShield Compliance Dashboard

## 1. Executive Summary
*   **Product Name:** RegShield (or ClearKYC)
*   **The Pitch:** A two-sided, AI-powered compliance platform that bridges the gap between independent advisors and LPL's back office. Advisors use a frictionless portal to upload raw client documents, while AWS Bedrock instantly synthesizes this unstructured data into an interactive, summarized "Compliance Dossier" for the back office. This turns a multi-day review bottleneck into a 3-minute approval, accelerating AUM growth.

## 2. Target User Personas
*   **User 1: The Independent Advisor (The Initiator):** Wants a dead-simple interface to drag-and-drop client documents (statements, IDs) and check if their client is "Cleared to Fund." They do not want to see complex compliance jargon.
*   **User 2: LPL Back-Office Reviewer (The Decision Maker):** Manages a queue of hundreds of onboarding requests. Wants to see a prioritized list ("Action Required", "Pending Review") and relies on AI to summarize the risk so they only have to manually review anomalies.

## 3. Core Features & Capabilities

### Feature 1: The Advisor Upload Portal
A streamlined, drag-and-drop interface where the advisor inputs basic client details and uploads unstructured documents (Driver's Licenses, Competitor Brokerage Statements, Trust Agreements). 

### Feature 2: The Bedrock "Compliance Dossier" (Interactive PDF)
*The Core AI Engine:* Once the advisor uploads the documents, AWS Bedrock analyzes the disparate files, extracts the necessary data, and generates a unified, beautifully formatted, and interactive PDF summary. 
*   *Use Case:* Instead of opening 5 different attachments, the Reviewer opens one AI-generated PDF that highlights Key Entities, Source of Funds, and flags any discrepancies (e.g., "AI Note: Client application states 'Joint', but uploaded Merrill Lynch statement is 'Individual'").

### Feature 3: The Back-Office Traffic Light Dashboard
A visual queue for the reviewer that categorizes the client's compliance status into four distinct regulatory buckets, scoring each as Green (Pass), Yellow (Manual Review Needed), or Red (Failed/Missing).
*   **Bucket A: Identity & AML**
*   **Bucket B: Reg BI & Suitability**
*   **Bucket C: Required Disclosures**
*   **Bucket D: Vulnerable Adult Protection**

### Feature 4: Smart Remediation Workflows
If the back-office reviewer finds an issue in the Bedrock summary, they can click "Request Clarification." The system auto-generates a secure, compliant SMS/email back to the advisor/client specifying exactly what is missing.

## 4. UI/UX Layout Guidelines

**View A: Advisor Portal (Frontend)**
*   **Layout:** Clean, minimalistic. A simple "New Client Onboarding" wizard.
*   **Components:** A large drag-and-drop zone for files, and a simple status tracker (e.g., "Submitted" -> "Pending Review" -> "Cleared to Fund").

**View B: Back-Office Reviewer Dashboard (Backend)**
*   **Left Navigation Bar:** Client List Queue (filtered by "Action Required," "Pending Review," "Cleared to Fund").
*   **Main Stage (Split Screen):**
    *   *Left Side:* The embedded, interactive AI-generated PDF "Compliance Dossier" from Bedrock.
    *   *Right Side:* The 4-Bucket Traffic Light Grid and the "Action Center" with Approve/Reject/Remediate buttons.

## 5. Data Inputs & Expected Outputs

**Inputs (What the system receives from Advisor):**
*   Basic Client Info (Name, proposed portfolio).
*   Unstructured Documents (PDF of competitor statement, image of ID).

**Outputs (What the system generates for Reviewer):**
*   An AI-synthesized PDF document summarizing the risk and contents of the uploads.
*   A normalized JSON payload of compliance scores feeding the Traffic Light UI.

## 6. Hackathon MVP Scope (The Demo Flow)
To win the hackathon, show the seamless handoff between the two personas:
1.  **Start as the Advisor:** Show the sleek upload screen. Drag and drop a messy 10-page competitor statement and a Driver's License for "John Doe." Hit submit.
2.  **The AI Loading Screen:** Briefly show a "Processing via AWS Bedrock..." animation.
3.  **Switch to the Reviewer View:** Open the Back-Office Dashboard. Show the queue with John Doe listed as "Pending Review."
4.  **The "Wow" Moment:** Click on John Doe. The screen splits. On the left, the interactive, 1-page PDF generated by Bedrock that perfectly summarizes the 10-page statement and ID. On the right, the Traffic Light system (all Green).
5.  **The Climax:** The presenter clicks "Approve." A massive "Cleared to Fund" badge appears. 
6.  *The Pitch:* "We just turned a 3-hour manual document review into a 10-second AI-assisted approval."
