CLIENT: Let me think about it. I'd want to talk to my wife first.
ADVISOR: Of course, no rush. And maybe keep it between us for now.
CLIENT: Okay. Back to my regular account, should I change anything there?
ADVISOR: No, I'd stay the course. Your mix matches the moderate risk level you told me you're comfortable with, and the fund costs are low.
CLIENT: Sounds good. Thanks.
